Watch on your TV with Kodi

Install the Promptioo TV add-on once. It lists every channel by category and language, and it switches to a working link by itself when one stops.

Download the Kodi add-on (.zip)

  1. In Kodi, open Settings > System > Add-ons and turn on Unknown sources.
  2. Open Add-ons > Install from zip file and choose the zip you downloaded. Or add as a file source and pick the zip there.
  3. Open Install from repository > Telugu Live Repository > Video add-ons > Telugu Live and press Install.
  4. When Kodi asks to load all channels into the TV section, answer Yes. Channels then appear under TV, grouped by language.

Playlist links

Paste a link into any IPTV player, or into Kodi's IPTV Simple Client.

Promptioo TV lists free public streams. Channels come and go, and some only play in India or in Kodi.
